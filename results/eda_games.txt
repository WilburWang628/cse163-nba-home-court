1. DATA SIZE
Raw games.csv: 26651 rows x 21 columns
Cleaned games: 24835 rows x 25 columns
Each row is one NBA game, with the home team's and the away team's box-score totals side by side.
Cleaning removed rows with missing scores, repeated GAME_IDs, preseason games and play-in games, and added GAME_TYPE, POINT_DIFF, BUBBLE and PERIOD.
Cleaned data covers 2003-10-28 to 2022-12-22 (SEASON 2003 to 2022).
