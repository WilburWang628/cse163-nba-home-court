RQ1 - H0 B: no trend in home win rate across normal seasons (slope = 0)
Test: statsmodels OLS, win_rate ~ SEASON, n = 16 seasons.
Slope = -0.00241 per season (-2.41 percentage points per decade), p = 0.001562, R^2 = 0.522

Assumption 1 - residuals roughly normal (Q-Q plot: figures/fig4_rq1_trend_diagnostics.png). Shapiro-Wilk p = 0.9915: OK.
Assumption 2 - no autocorrelation in residuals. Durbin-Watson = 2.404 (about 2 = none; below 1.5 or above 2.5 is a warning): OK.
Note: seasons 2019 and 2020 are missing, so 2018 and 2021 are treated as neighbors.
Assumption 3 - linear relationship (residual plot in the same figure). p-value of an added SEASON^2 term = 0.08074: OK.
Influence check: largest Cook's distance = 1.213 (SEASON 2021); rule-of-thumb cutoff 4/n = 0.250: NOT MET.
Without SEASON 2021: slope = -0.00156, p = 0.01676.

RQ2 - Permutation tests (for H0 C and H0 D)
Method: pool the two groups, shuffle the PERIOD labels 10000 times, and count how often the shuffled difference in means is at least as extreme as the observed one (two-sided, seed 163).
Why: it does not assume normality, a large sample, or equal variances. Its only assumption is exchangeability: under H0 the period label does not matter, which requires independent games. This makes it a good check for the small bubble groups (88 and 83 games), where the z-test and t-test rely on large-sample approximations.
